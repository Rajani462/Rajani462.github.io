About the work

Research

My research centers on understanding precipitation processes, extreme rainfall, and hydroclimatic variability using satellite observations, model–data integration, and statistical analysis.

  • Precipitation evaluation and scaling
  • Diurnal variability of rainfall
  • Hydrological impacts of extreme events
Satellite precipitation (IMERG / GPM) SWOT river discharge Hydrologic modeling Extreme value statistics
